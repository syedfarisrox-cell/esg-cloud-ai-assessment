# Q4 NotebookLM test protocol (about 30 minutes)

**Scenario:** You are a student preparing a briefing for a governance and compliance workshop on Australia's mandatory climate-related financial disclosures. NotebookLM is your research assistant.

Record each step in `logs/experiment_log.md` as you go, and take a screenshot at every camera icon (📸).

## Step 0: Set up (5 min)
1. Go to notebooklm.google.com and click **Create new notebook**. Name it `ESG Reporting Briefing`.
2. Add these sources (use **Website** for links, or upload PDFs):
   - AASB S2 Climate-related Disclosures: https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-s2-dec-2025 (or the PDF linked on that page)
   - ASIC Regulatory Guide 280 *Sustainability reporting*: search "ASIC RG 280 sustainability reporting" and add the asic.gov.au page
   - IFRS S2 Climate-related Disclosures: search "IFRS S2 climate-related disclosures" and add the ifrs.org page
   - If a website won't load as a source, use NotebookLM's **Discover sources** button with the same search words instead, and note that in your log.
   - Optional: one of your own BUS5001 lecture slides on ESG or cloud analytics (PDF)
3. 📸 The notebook showing the source list.

## Step 1: Source-grounded Q&A with citations (5 min)
Ask in the chat:
- *"Which Australian entities must prepare climate-related disclosures, and from what date does each group start?"*
- *"What does AASB S2 require for Scope 3 emissions, and is there any transition relief?"*

For each answer, click **two citation numbers** and check that the quoted source text really supports the claim. Record each as correct, partly correct or unsupported.
📸 One answer with a citation open.

## Step 2: Hallucination probe (3 min)
Ask something the sources do **not** contain:
- *"What penalty did ASIC impose on Woolworths in 2025 for breaching AASB S2?"*

Record whether it says the sources don't cover this (good) or invents an answer (hallucination).
📸 The answer.

## Step 3: Report / Briefing doc (5 min)
Go to **Studio**, then **Reports**, then **Briefing doc** (or **Study guide**).
Pick **three factual claims** from it and verify them against the sources. Note anything missing, oversimplified or wrong.
📸 The top of the report.

## Step 4: Audio Overview (5 min, generation runs in the background)
In **Studio**, choose **Audio Overview** and click **Generate**. Start this early, because it can take a few minutes.
Listen to about 2 minutes. Note: how accurate it is, whether it oversimplifies, its tone, and how long it took to generate.
📸 The audio player.

## Step 5: Mind map (2 min)
In **Studio**, choose **Mind map**. Does the structure match how the topic is really organised?
📸 The mind map.

## Step 6: Flashcards or Quiz (5 min)
In **Studio**, choose **Flashcards** or **Quiz**. Answer 5 questions and note any question that is wrong, ambiguous or trivial.
📸 One quiz question.

## Notes to capture for the analysis
- Did every answer stay inside the sources? Were the citations precise (exact passage) or vague?
- What did it leave out or get wrong?
- How long did each feature take compared with doing it manually?
- Privacy: would you upload confidential company documents? (Check your account type: personal or university Workspace.)
